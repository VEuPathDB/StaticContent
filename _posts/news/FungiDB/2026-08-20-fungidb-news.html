MAJOR UPDATES IN FungiDB 71

Release 71 marks an important milestone for VEuPathDB/ FungiDB, as our production websites are now running on new software infrastructure. These changes – nearly two years in the making and piloted on our beta-release sites over the past year – are intended to improve the long-term performance, sustainability, and cost-efficiency of VEuPathDB resources. Because of the scale and scope of these changes, production sites will continue to carry the ‘beta’ designation for several months, as we monitor site performance and usability.

Please Contact Us to report anything that you find difficult to locate or use, or that appears to be working improperly. Also note that the previous VEuPathDB release remains available at https://legacy.veupathdb.org/.

New & updated features include-

  • Key changes in infrastructure, including migration from Oracle to PostgreSQL, providing a more flexible and sustainable foundation for our databases. In addition, all genome and proteome workflows have been converted to publicly available Nextflow workflows, making VEuPathDB data-processing pipelines more reproducible, transparent, and easier to maintain and improve.
  • The VEuPathDB portal (VEuPathDB.org) now provides full access to all gene pages, variant searches, and JBrowse views for all component sites, allowing unified exploration of data across all VEuPathDB resources.
  • Product descriptions have been updated for >2.3 million genes (~25% of the database), taking advantage of ARBA rules (adapted from UniProt), InterPro domain searches, and taxonomy to replace outdated or uninformative descriptions (e.g., “hypothetical protein”) with high confidence assessment of protein functions, pathways, and/or subcellular location.
    • Want to see what’s changed? From the Download directory, you can obtain a list of all updated Gene Product Descriptions (and their previous descriptions from release 68), for each genome supported by VEuPathDB (look for “gene_product_updates_b71.txt”).
    • Note that manually curated sources always take priority over automatic annotations. Please Contact Us or add a User Comment to further improve product descriptions or let us know of any erroneous changes!
  • My Datasets: Import your own data into VEuPathDB. It is now easier to upload your own datasets, seamlessly integrated with other information in VEuPathDB, letting you explore data privately and share with collaborators, in preparation for broader public release.
    • Supported datatypes include raw or normalized counts from RNA-seq experiments (along with tools for querying differential expression), phenotype data, generic data tables, bigWig files, gene lists, etc.
    • Validation helps identify problems with RNA-seq and bigWig files before upload, an updated “Help” tab guides users through preparing and uploading data, and an AI Metadata Analyzer is available for RNA-Seq raw count datasets.
    • Incorporate detailed metadata, making public datasets easier to discover, understand, and reuse (essential metadata is required for public release).
    • Users control sharing of their datasets with collaborators, and public release, advancing scientific discovery in compliance with funder & journal requirements.
  • Improved variant analysis. SNP records are now represented as Short Variants (including both SNPs & indels), providing a more complete view of genetic variation. The updated analysis pipeline uses modern alignment and variant-calling tools, VCF-based tracks in JBrowse make it easier to explore variants in their genomic context, and a new per-gene statistics search provides more detailed summaries of variation across genes.
  • A new User-Comment interface makes it easier to contribute evidence and update information about individual genes. AI-assisted drafting helps you turn a short note or uploaded PDF into well-formed annotations visible to the research community.
  • Web Services Access. Users can now access their personal VEuPathDB API key directly from their user account, enabling programmatic access to VEuPathDB data and the ability to build multi-step searches, analyze data, and download results through the REST API.
  • Organism Presets. Save a set of organisms you use often for convenient use in any search. A new option also permits expanding the tree to display your selections in phylogenetic context.
  • Additional data and analysis updates: This release also includes refreshed Gene Ontology, InterPro, SignalP, and IEDB epitope annotations, BUSCO assessments across all organisms, and improvements in sample-detail and bulk RNA-seq searches, including new notebook-style interfaces.

    • 150 New Datasets have been added in releases 69, 70 & 71, including 48 genomes & annotation, 67 RNA-seq experiments, and 35 other datasets. Datasets added in release 71 are listed below (see prior news items for datasets from previous releases).
